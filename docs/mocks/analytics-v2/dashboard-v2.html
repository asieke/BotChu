About these numbers
Spend
$ is what OpenRouter billed. ≈$ is the market-equivalent price of the tokens a subscription CLI reported, from the public price list dated Oct 1, 2026. A model with no price is never counted as $0: it shows as —, and its runs are counted under Unpriced.
Cache hit rate
Cache-read tokens ÷ (input + cache read + cache write).
Per finished task
Spend on a finished task's turns and sub-agent runs ÷ finished tasks.
Wasted spend
Spend on failed and stopped turns and runs. Retried is the part whose task ran again afterwards.
Efficiency
Model swaps price the same tokens at the other model's list price. Cache gaps price a bot's uncached input at the other bots' cache hit rate.
Days, durations and history
Calendar days in America/New_York. Unknown durations are left out of medians. Job histories keep their latest 50 runs, so older periods may show less than happened.
